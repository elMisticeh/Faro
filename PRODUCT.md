# PRODUCT.md

## Product
CAMPO — Plataforma de inteligencia de mercado inmobiliario para Torreón, Coahuila.

## Purpose
Enable real estate analysts at COEZA to explore property listings and demographic zone data together, connecting scraped market data with INEGI census data to inform investment and product strategy.

## Users
Real estate analysts and directors. Small team (2-5), high analytical fluency. Work at desktops and tablets during project evaluation and client presentations. Dim professional office environment, high information density needed.

## Register
product

## Tone
Professional, dense, data-forward. Information over decoration. The interface disappears into the analysis.

## Anti-references
- Consumer real estate apps (Casas y Terrenos, Lamudi)
- Marketing dashboards with gauge charts and KPI cards everywhere
- Overly colorful BI tools with rainbow charts
- Neon-on-black crypto trading UIs
